Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for mobile games developed by Rishi Vagadiya (we, us, or our), including CrowdWar: Crowd Runner 3D, Car Parking Game 3D, and related titles. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how information is handled.

Information Collection & Use

Our applications are free-to-play games supported by advertisements. To provide and optimize our game services, third-party SDKs integrated into our games may collect non-personal device information, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID, Android ID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • Gameplay events, crash reports, and performance analytics
  • Device model, operating system version, and system language

Third-Party Services & Ad Networks

Our games integrate third-party SDKs that have their own privacy policies regarding data usage:

  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google Advertising Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Used for achievements, leaderboards, and basic app integrity. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Used to understand game crashes, stabilize performance, and improve player retention.

Advertising & Opt-Out

Advertisers may use advertising identifiers to deliver personalized advertisements. You can reset or limit ad personalization on your Android device at any time by visiting:
Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our games are designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can delete such records.

Data Security

We value your trust in using our games. While no method of digital transmission or storage is 100% secure, we utilize commercially standard protocols provided by trusted partners (Google and Unity) to protect all transmission data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We advise you to review this page occasionally for any changes. Any modifications are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
